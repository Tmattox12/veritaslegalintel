Decision-Making Structure

Core custody terms

Sole Legal Decision-Making

Use when one parent needs exclusive authority over education, medical or mental-health decisions, with notice rights and document access preserved to the other parent.

Joint Legal Decision-Making

Use when both parents can participate in major decisions, with a tie-break protocol, response deadlines and emergency exceptions defined in the order.

Final Say / Tie-Breaker

Assign a final decision-maker by category only after a required meet-and-confer period, written notice and a documented attempt to resolve the issue.

Emergency Authority

Allow either parent to act immediately for urgent safety or medical issues, with a same-day notice obligation and post-event documentation requirement.
