How a new matter flows
  1. 1 · Case Intake — create the matter (case-intake.html). This makes it the active matter.
  2. 2 · Import Documents — drag & drop below. Files auto-categorize into the buckets on this page.
  3. 3 · Auto-parse — bank / card statements become transaction lists (see View Parsed Transactions / CSV export).
  4. 4 · Clear & allocate — map expenses to AFI lines in the Expense Hub; assign income to each party in the Income Hub.
  5. 5 · Track completeness — the Document Checklist (bottom) shows what Rule 49 items are still missing.
