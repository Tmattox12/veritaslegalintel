💡 Settlement Considerations
Fairness Analysis:
✓ Each party's total assets received vs. obligations
✓ Monthly cash flow impact (after support/debt)
✓ Long-term financial security
✓ Tax implications of different divisions

Feasibility Check:
✓ Can Party A afford to pay support?
✓ Can Party B survive on assets + support?
✓ Are debts assigned to paying party?
✓ Is cash flow sustainable post-divorce?

Key Terms:
• Asset Division (%) vs. Support ($ amount)
• Child Support vs. Spousal Support balance
• Debt assignment impact on net worth
• Tax-efficient vs. tax-inefficient divisions
