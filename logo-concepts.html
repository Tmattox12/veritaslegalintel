🎯 Recommended: Sigma in Ring (Σ●)

Why: The Sigma (Σ) symbolizes mathematical sum/analysis and financial rigor, while the enclosing ring represents security and verification. It's distinctive, memorable, and immediately conveys "verified financial analysis." The current Æ is elegant but doesn't differentiate Veritas in the legal tech space. Sigma is instantly recognizable and brand-forward.

Design Next Steps: Create a polished icon version with a security ring, suitable for 16px favicon through 1000px hero logo. Consider color variants (blue, gold, black).
