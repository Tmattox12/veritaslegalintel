🎯 Imputation Methodologies

When to use each approach
1. Documented Income Approach
Use tax returns, W-2s, paystubs, 1099s as filed. Most conservative. Used when income is clearly documented and verifiable.
2. Average Historical Income
Average last 2–3 years of tax returns or pay history. Accounts for variability in bonuses, commissions, self-employment. Often used for business owners & commission-based employees.
3. Earning Capacity / Vocational Expert
Used when party is voluntarily underemployed, unemployed, or claims limited earning capacity. Expert determines what they could earn (not what they claim).
4. Business Income Analysis
For self-employed: reconstruct from profit & loss statements, bank deposits, tax returns. Adjust for add-backs (depreciation, non-recurring expenses, perks).
5. Cash Flow / Bank Deposit Analysis
When documentation is poor: analyze bank deposits to identify undisclosed income. Used when party hides self-employment or informal income streams.
