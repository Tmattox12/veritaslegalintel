🎯 Imputation Methodologies (Reference)
1. Documented Income
Tax returns, W-2s, paystubs as filed. Most conservative. When income is clearly documented and verifiable.
2. Historical Average
Average 2–3 years of tax returns. For variable income (bonus, commission, self-employment).
3. Earning Capacity
Vocational expert assessment. When party is voluntarily underemployed or unemployed.
4. Business Income
Reconstruct from P&L, bank deposits, tax returns. Adjust for add-backs and discretionary expenses.
5. Bank Deposit Analysis
When documentation is poor: analyze 12-month deposits to identify undisclosed income streams.
