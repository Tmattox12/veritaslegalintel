⚖ Arizona Legal Framework

A.R.S. § 25-320 · Income-Shares Model
Guideline Percentages by Children: 1 child = 17% of combined AGI; 2 children = 25%; 3 = 32%; 4 = 40%; 5+ = 50%. Each parent contributes proportional to income share. Guideline is rebuttable presumption; courts may deviate based on statutory factors including parenting time disparity, standard of living, and special needs.

Parenting Time Impact on Support:

Obligor Parenting Time Adjustment Factor
Less than 10% No reduction (pay full guideline)
10% to 30% Minor adjustment may apply
More than 30% May warrant downward deviation
50% (50/50 custody) Significant deviation / offset likely
