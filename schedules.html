Exchange Logistics

Operational details
  • 1
    School-year exchanges

    Default at school dismissal so neither parent needs direct face-to-face contact unless school is closed.

  • 2
    No-school day fallback

    Use a fixed neutral site, exact time and 15-minute grace rule, with notice required for late arrival.

  • 3
    Transportation allocation

    Specify who picks up, who returns, and whether activity transport follows possession or enrollment parent.

  • 4
    Belongings / medication / devices

    List mandatory transfer items so conflicts over uniforms, medicine, homework or chargers do not repeat.

Drafting Reminders

  • Define whether holiday time overrides the base weekly pattern.
  • Set exact exchange times, not vague “afternoon” language.
  • Include notice windows for camps, vacations and out-of-state travel.
  • Clarify what happens when school is not in session.

Deadline Calendar

Case management
  • 30 d
    Summer election notice

    Parent claiming summer blocks gives written proposed dates with a conflict-resolution deadline.

  • 14 d
    Holiday itinerary notice

    Addresses, flight details, emergency contacts, and any third-party caregiver information.

  • 72 h
    Non-emergency medical notice

    Upcoming appointments, specialist referrals, therapy changes or medication updates.

  • 24 h
    Schedule-change request

    Advance notice for swaps when possible, with a written acceptance requirement to avoid ambiguity.
