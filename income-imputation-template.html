🎯 Imputation Methodologies

Reference guide for selection rationale
1. Documented Income Approach
Use tax returns (1040, Schedules C/E), W-2s, and paystubs as filed or submitted. This is the most conservative and defensible approach when documentation is complete and income is stable.
2. Average Historical Income
Average the last 2–3 years of tax returns or pay history. Useful for self-employed, commission-based, or bonus-eligible employees whose income varies year to year. Smooths volatility while remaining conservative.
3. Earning Capacity / Vocational Expert
Applied when a party is voluntarily underemployed, unemployed, or claims limited earning capacity despite documented work history. Expert determines what they could earn with their education, experience, and skills.
4. Business Income Analysis
For self-employed parties: reconstruct from profit & loss statements, bank deposits, tax returns, and contract records. Adjust for add-backs (depreciation, discretionary expenses, personal perks) to arrive at discretionary income available for support.
5. Cash Flow / Bank Deposit Analysis
When documentation is limited or a party is suspected of hiding income: analyze 12-month bank deposits to identify undisclosed income streams. Accounts for informal employment, cash businesses, and side income not reported on tax returns.
