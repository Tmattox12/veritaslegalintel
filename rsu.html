Method notes — verify before filing

Formulas (time rule)

  • Hug (In re Marriage of Hug, 154 Cal. App. 3d 780 (1984)): (date of service − hire date) ÷ (vest date − hire date). Treats the award as pay for service since hire; gives the larger community share.
  • Nelson (In re Marriage of Nelson, 177 Cal. App. 3d 150 (1986)): (date of service − grant date) ÷ (vest date − grant date). Treats the award as pay for future service.
  • If the start date is before the marriage, the numerator starts at the date of marriage. A tranche that vested during the marriage and before service is 100% community when the start date is after the marriage.
  • Grants made after the date of service are separate (0%) unless shown to reward pre-service work.

Arizona

Community ends on service of a petition that results in a decree (A.R.S. § 25-211(A)(2)); community property is divided equitably, usually equally (§ 25-318(A)). No Arizona statute picks Hug or Nelson; courts have looked to these California cases. Confirm current authority.

Watch for

  • Double counting: post-service vests are in the account and also on the W-2. Divide them once, and address whether the same shares are also counted as income for support.
  • Taxes: shares are delivered net of withholding (Table 1, − Tax withheld). The gain reserve estimates tax on the unrealized gain if the shares are sold.
  • Unvested shares: divide "if, as, and when vested," in shares.
