Formula selection

The correct formula depends on the award's purpose and governing jurisdiction.

  • Future service: measures the marital part of service between grant and separation.
  • Past service: measures service from employment start to separation against employment start to vest.

Obtain legal review before relying on a classification or allocation.

Documentation checklist

  • Separation or service date
  • Share count and valuation evidence
