⚖ Arizona Legal Framework

A.R.S. § 25-319(F)
Statutory Guideline: 20% of the payor's income minus the payee's income, subject to duration limits based on marriage length (30%–100% of marriage duration). Arizona presumes this amount is appropriate, but courts may deviate based on statutory factors: standard of living, ability to pay, health, age, earning capacity, and property division.

Duration Caps by Marriage Length:

Marriage Duration Maintenance Duration Cap
Less than 1 year No spousal maintenance ordered
1 to 3 years 30% of marriage duration
3 to 10 years 35% of marriage duration
10 to 15 years 40% of marriage duration
15 to 20 years 50% of marriage duration
20+ years Indefinite (no cap)
